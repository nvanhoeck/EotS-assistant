This helper helps the player go easier through the rules with interactive overview, brief or better visualized rules, and more bundled rules for things like India, China, US, ... The interactive rules also has references to the rulebook sections when coming with a statement, but also to other parts of the helper (eg: "when in supply" has an overview in the helper itself, which can be clicked on).
The rules helper should have go back buttons, so that when clicked 5 times through, you can press 5 times back to go back where you came from.

Most structure is accordion style. The main sections are chapter which you can click on to go to the next page. Once there, you can click on different sections, which open op the rules.
